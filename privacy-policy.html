This privacy policy applies to the Countless app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Raitis Šaripo (collectively, the "Application"). Raitis Šaripo is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


What stays on your device

Countless is designed to work without an account. Your push-up sessions and the individual reps within them, your totals, streaks and personal records, your challenges and achievements, the body weight you enter, and your settings are stored on your device, in the Application's own local database.

None of this content is sent to the Service Provider. There is no Countless account, and the Service Provider cannot see how many push-ups you do, when you did them, or what you weigh. The only exceptions are described in the sections below.


No accounts and no cloud sync

The Application does not ask you to register, sign in, or provide a name, email address or phone number. It has no server of its own, and your session history is not synced or backed up to the Service Provider. Your data lives on the device it was recorded on, and it travels with that device's own iCloud or iTunes backup if you have one, which is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Deleting the Application deletes the data it holds.


Motion from your headphones

Countless counts your push-ups by reading the motion sensor in compatible headphones, such as AirPods, using Apple's Core Motion framework. iOS asks for your permission before this can start, and you can withdraw it at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness.

Motion is processed live on your device, entirely within the Application, and is used only to decide when a rep has happened. The raw motion stream is not recorded, not stored, and not transmitted anywhere. What remains after a set is the count itself and the timing of each rep, saved locally as described above. The model name of the connected headphones is used to show which device is counting; see "Information Collection and Use" for the one way it is also included in analytics.


Audio and haptics

The Application plays a short tick into your headphones at the bottom of each rep, and can vibrate your phone. To keep counting reliably while your screen is off or the Application is in the background, it holds an active audio playback session. The Application does not use the microphone, does not record audio, and does not listen to anything.


Apple Health (optional)

Countless can save each finished session to Apple Health as a workout. This is off until you turn it on in Settings, iOS asks for your permission separately, and you can turn it off again at any time.

When it is on:

  • The Application writes a functional strength training workout for the session, with its start and end time, and an estimate of active energy burned. Nothing else is written.
  • The permission is write-only. The Application does not read anything out of Apple Health — the body weight used for the energy estimate is the one you enter in Countless, not one read from Health.
  • Data in Apple Health is stored on your device by Apple and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. It is not sent to the Service Provider, and the Service Provider has no access to it.
  • Deleting a session in Countless also removes the workout it wrote, and editing a session's rep count updates it. You can also delete anything it has written directly in the Health app, and revoke the permission in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects analytics and crash information when you download and use it, in order to find bugs and understand which features are used. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • The screens of the Application that you open, and when
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The mobile operating system and device model you use
  • The model name of the headphones you count with, and whether you have a subscription
  • An app instance identifier generated for analytics purposes
  • Diagnostic information about a crash, if the Application crashes

Analytics events record which action happened — for example that a session started, that a session was ended manually, or that a purchase was completed — and never how you performed. Your rep counts, session durations, session times, records, streaks, challenges and body weight are not included in analytics or in crash reports. This is enforced by the Application's structure rather than by policy alone: the fixed list of events it is able to send has no room to carry such a value. Crash reports likewise carry the name of what the Application was doing, not the numbers involved.


Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

Countless does not collect the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), does not link the advertising framework, does not display advertising, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases

Countless Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. Payment is handled by Apple and the Service Provider never receives or sees your payment details. Whether you are subscribed is determined on your device from Apple's own signed transaction records; there is no purchase account or server held by the Service Provider, and restoring a purchase on another device is handled by Apple.


Opt-Out Rights

You can stop further collection of information from your mobile device by uninstalling the Application. Uninstalling will stop the Application from collecting data from your device, but it does not automatically delete information that has already been transmitted to the Service Provider or to third parties. Uninstalling does not remove workouts the Application already wrote to Apple Health; delete those in the Health app.

To request deletion of your personal data, to withdraw consent, or to exercise any of your rights, contact the Service Provider at saripo.raitis@gmail.com.


Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains personal data based on its necessity for the stated purposes:

  • User Provided Data: Retained for the duration of your use of the Application plus 12 months thereafter, unless longer retention is required by law
  • Automatically Collected Data: Retained for up to 24 months from collection, unless longer retention is required for legal compliance
  • Aggregated and Anonymized Data: Retained indefinitely as it no longer identifies you
  • Data required for legal compliance: Retained as long as required by applicable law

You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligation to retain it.
