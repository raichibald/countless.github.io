Counting accuracy and compatibility

The Application counts repetitions by interpreting motion data from the sensor in compatible headphones. Counts, session timings, and the estimate of active energy burned are approximations derived from that sensor. They can be wrong: reps can be missed, movement that is not a push-up can be counted, and the energy estimate is a calculation based on the body weight you enter, not a measurement. The Service Provider does not warrant that any count, statistic, record, streak, or estimate produced by the Application is accurate, and you can correct a session's count yourself in the Application.

Automatic counting requires an iPhone running a supported version of iOS together with headphones that contain a motion sensor, and requires you to grant the Motion & Fitness permission. Not all headphones include such a sensor, and the Service Provider cannot guarantee that any particular device, headphone model, firmware version or operating system version will work, or will continue to work after a future update by Apple or another manufacturer. Where automatic counting is unavailable, sets can be entered manually.
