How it works

Your head moves with every rep. Countless listens.

  1. 1

    Put your AirPods in

    Anything with a motion sensor works: AirPods Pro, AirPods 3 and later, AirPods Max, Beats Fit Pro, Powerbeats Pro 2.

  2. 2

    Tap Start, get down

    One tap, then just do push-ups. The phone can stay in your pocket.

  3. 3

    Hear the tick

    A quiet tick at the bottom of every rep and a marker on every tenth. Stop moving and the set finishes on its own.

FAQ

Good questions.

Which headphones do I need?

AirPods Pro (any generation), AirPods 3rd generation and later, AirPods Max, Beats Fit Pro or Powerbeats Pro 2. AirPods 1st and 2nd generation don’t have a motion sensor and can’t count. You also need to allow Motion & Fitness access when iOS asks.

Does it keep counting with the screen off?

Yes. Countless holds an audio session so it can keep counting while the screen is off or the app is in the background. That’s also why you hear the tick in your headphones.

How accurate is the count?

Counts come from head motion, so they’re an approximation: a rep can be missed, or other movement can be counted. If it’s off, fix the count in one tap after the set.

Does it use the camera or microphone?

No. It reads the motion sensor in your headphones. The only audio is the short tick it plays for you.

Do I need an account? Where is my data?

No account. Your sets are stored on your device only and aren’t synced, so they travel with your device’s own iCloud or iTunes backup if you have one. Deleting the app deletes the data it holds.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the iOS Settings app, tap your name → Subscriptions, or manage it in the App Store. Cancelling stops future renewals, and access continues until the period you’ve paid for ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
